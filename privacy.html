WiseRealm

Privacy Policy

WiseRealm is a classroom review game made by a teacher for use with their students. This page explains, in plain language, what information WiseRealm keeps and why. Last updated November 2026.

What we store

Students can play every game as a guest without signing in. If a student signs in with their school Google account, WiseRealm stores only:

  • Their Google account email address
  • Their first name and last initial (for example “Maya L.”), taken from their Google profile
  • The pixel hero they design, their coins, the shop items they own, and a history of coins earned and spent

WiseRealm never sees or stores passwords. Sign-in is handled by Google.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, no tracking across other websites, and no selling or sharing of student information.
  • Game answers are used only to run the live game and are not saved after the game ends.
  • There is no chat between students.

Who can see it

A student can see their own profile. The teacher can see students’ names, emails and coin balances to run the class reward system. Nobody else can.

Where it is kept

Information is stored securely with Supabase, our database provider. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Removing information

A student or parent can ask the teacher to delete a student’s WiseRealm account at any time, and it will be removed along with its coins and items.

Questions

Please contact the classroom teacher who uses WiseRealm with your class.
