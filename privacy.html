WiseRealm

For families: what is WiseRealm?

WiseRealm is a review game website made by a teacher for their own classes. Students answer questions about what they’re learning, and their pixel hero fights a boss, runs to the right corner, digs for treasure or escapes a maze. Right answers earn coins, which they spend on outfits and fun emotes for their hero.

At home, students can use the Practice page to study the question sets their teacher assigned, with four single-player study games. Practice is limited to 50 coins a day, so there’s no reason to play for hours.

The short version on privacy:

  • Students sign in with their school Google account. WiseRealm never sees their password.
  • Other students only see a student’s hero and the in-game name they choose. They never see real names or emails.
  • Only the student’s own teachers see their real name, email and results, to keep track of their learning.
  • No ads. No tracking. Nothing is sold or shared. There is no chat between students.
  • Students can delete their own account at any time (Profile → Delete my account and data), or you can ask their teacher to do it.

Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, what information WiseRealm keeps, who can see it and how to remove it. Last updated November 2026.

Playing as a guest

Anyone can play a live class game without signing in. A guest types a name and designs a hero. That name and hero are only used while the game is running and are not saved anywhere after it ends. Guests don’t earn coins.

We do not collect passwords, birthdays, addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, or contacts. Students can’t send messages to each other.

Who can see what

  • The student sees their own profile, hero, coins, results and practice progress.
  • Other students see only heroes, in-game names and game scores on the class screen while playing. In the class village, classmates in the same class can look at each other’s houses (with the in-game name and hero). Students in other classes can’t. There are no messages, comments or reactions on houses.
  • The student’s own teachers (the teachers whose class they joined or whose game they played) see their real name, email, coins, results and practice progress. Other teachers who use WiseRealm cannot see them.
  • The site’s administrator (the teacher who runs WiseRealm) can technically access the database to keep it running, and doesn’t use it for anything else.

Who else is involved

  • Google handles sign-in with the student’s school account.
  • Supabase stores the database and passes live game messages between the class screen and the students’ devices. Live game messages (answers, arrow presses, emotes) are delivered and not stored.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Like any web host, it may keep short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

All other code and fonts are stored on wiserealm.org itself, so no other companies are contacted when students use the site. WiseRealm has no ads, analytics, tracking cookies or social media buttons.

Saved on the device

The browser keeps a few things on the device itself: the sign-in session, the hero and name a guest last used, and a random ID so a student who reloads during a game keeps their score. Teachers’ question sets are also saved on their computer. None of these are used for tracking.

How game security works

Each live game has a 5-digit code. Account information is never sent over the live game connection. Instead, the class screen looks up who is playing through the database using the teacher’s login. Teachers can lock a room once everyone has joined, remove anyone from a game, and require students to be signed in.

Deleting information

  • Students can delete their whole account at any time: Profile → My privacy → Delete my account and data. This removes their hero, coins, items, house, results, practice progress and class memberships.
  • Parents and guardians can ask the student’s teacher, who can delete the student’s data from the class page.
  • Data is kept while the account exists. Teachers may also delete accounts at the end of the school year.

Questions

Please contact your child’s teacher who uses WiseRealm with their class.
