WiseRealm

The short version (for students)

  • WiseRealm is a free review game for your classes. Play to learn and have fun!
  • Be kind. Pick a friendly name. Don’t tease, copy someone else’s name, or upset others with emotes.
  • Play fair. Don’t try to cheat, break the game, or get into anyone else’s account.
  • Coins and items are just for fun inside WiseRealm. They aren’t real money and can’t be traded or sold.
  • Your teacher can see your results to help you learn. Other students only see your hero and game name.
  • If something goes wrong or feels wrong, tell your teacher.

1. What WiseRealm is

WiseRealm is a free, non-commercial educational project: review games, practice games and a class village for school classes. It has no ads and nothing to buy. It is run by the WiseRealm project, not by a school district or a company.

2. Who may use it

  • Teachers use WiseRealm with their own classes. Teacher accounts are added by the site administrator.
  • Students use WiseRealm as part of their classes, under their teacher’s supervision, and may also practice at home. Students under 18 should use it only as directed by their teacher or school.
  • Schools that use WiseRealm are responsible for making sure that its use fits their own policies and the laws that apply to them, including any notices or consent needed for students and families.

3. Accounts

  • Sign in only with your own school Google account, and don’t share it or use anyone else’s.
  • Guests may play live games without an account; nothing about a guest is saved after the game.
  • You can delete your account at any time (Profile → My privacy). Teachers can remove students from their classes and games.

4. Acceptable use

When using WiseRealm, you must not:

  • use rude, hateful, sexual or bullying names, or names that pretend to be someone else (a name filter helps with this, and teachers can rename or remove players);
  • harass, bully or upset other players;
  • cheat, interfere with games, or try to break, overload or get around how WiseRealm works;
  • try to see, change or delete anyone else’s data or account;
  • put other people’s personal information (such as addresses or phone numbers) into names, question sets or anywhere else;
  • use WiseRealm for anything unlawful, or to advertise or sell things.

Teachers and the site administrator may remove names, content or accounts that break these rules.

5. Teachers’ question sets

  • Teachers keep ownership of the questions they write.
  • By adding a question set, a teacher lets WiseRealm store it and show it to the students they share it with, so the games can work.
  • Teachers are responsible for having the right to use what they upload. For example, don’t copy large amounts from textbooks or other copyrighted material without permission, and don’t include students’ personal information.

Coins, shop items, emotes, furniture and houses exist only inside WiseRealm. They have no money value, can’t be bought, sold, traded or exchanged for anything outside WiseRealm, and may be changed, rebalanced or reset (for example, when a game is updated or an account is deleted).
