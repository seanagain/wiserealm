Paste from a spreadsheet

In Google Sheets (or a Flippity sheet), select your cells and copy them (⌘C / Ctrl+C), then paste here. Column 1 is the question or word, column 2 is the correct answer or definition. Any extra columns are treated as wrong answers. With only two columns, the game borrows the other answers as wrong choices. You can also type one per line like word - definition.
